Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This policy explains how Drupux (drupux.com and app.drupux.com), operated by Source Quarter LLC, collects, uses and protects the personal data of the people who use the service.

What we collect

  • Account data: name, email address and profile picture. If you sign in with Google, we receive only that basic profile information — never your password.
  • Business data: what you enter in the app, such as contacts, products, invoices, payments, members and employees.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type and activity logs needed to run and protect the service.

How we use it

  • To create your account, sign you in and give you access to the businesses you belong to.
  • To provide the service: the modules you turn on, public links you share (documents, statements, sign-up pages) and reports.
  • To provide support, keep the service secure, prevent abuse and meet legal obligations.

We don't sell personal data or use it for advertising.

Google user data

Drupux's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use your name, email and picture only to identify you in the app.

Who we share it with

We use providers that process data on our behalf, only to run the service: app hosting, database, email delivery and Google sign-in. When you invite someone (for example a teammate or your accountant) to your business, they can see that business's data according to the role you give them. We may also disclose data when the law requires it.

Your customers' data

The data about your customers, members and employees belongs to you; we process it on your behalf to provide the service. You are responsible for having a valid basis to record it. If one of them wants to exercise their rights, they can contact you or us.

How long we keep it

While your account is active. If you close it, we delete or anonymize your data, except what we must keep for legal, accounting or tax obligations.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, export or delete your personal data by writing to support@drupux.com.

Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), role-based access control and activity logs. No system is perfect, but we work to protect your data.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the current version. If changes are significant, we'll let you know in the app or by email.
